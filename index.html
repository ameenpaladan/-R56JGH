01 — About

I make images move, and moments last.

I am a multidisciplinary visual creative specializing in graphic design, visual effects, video editing, motion design, videography and creative content production.

My work begins with a simple belief: every frame should say something. I approach each project as a visual storyteller — combining design thinking with technical craft, from composing a single image to compositing a full cinematic shot.

Behind the screen and behind the lens, I am driven by a passion for filmmaking and the quiet detail that makes visuals feel cinematic.
